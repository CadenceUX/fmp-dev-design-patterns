## Fix made during the run

Opus's first `name-script` answer flagged two gaps in the pack's `naming.md`: whether the
first version of an action carries a variant once a second exists, and whether parameters
belong in the name (the Baseline puts the signature there). The pack now says every version
carries a variant when there is more than one, parameters go on the header's `PARAMETERS`
line, and trigger wrappers use the `Trigger` domain. Haiku re-checked after the fix:
`Email: Invoice To Contact | Single`, `… | Batch`, `Trigger: Invoice Detail OnRecordLoad`,
parameters on the `PARAMETERS` line. All correct.

Other existing cases (`write-script-no-adk`, `global-state`, `ai-step`, `skill-update-check`)
were spot-checked by reading, not re-run: their instructions changed only by ADT → ADK.
